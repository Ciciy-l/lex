Privacy is explicit.

隐私边界明确可见。

Lex releases keep upstream TapDB analytics disabled by default. Diagnostic logs stay local unless you upload them; crash auto-upload is a separate opt-in and is off by default. Cindy account traffic remains governed by Cindy's terms and privacy policy.

Lex 发行包默认关闭上游 TapDB 统计。诊断日志默认留在本机；崩溃自动上传是独立选项且默认关闭。Cindy 账号流量仍遵循 Cindy 官方条款与隐私政策。
